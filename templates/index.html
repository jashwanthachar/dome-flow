Tumakuru Municipal Civic Grievance Portal

Empowering Citizens for a Cleaner, Safer Tumakuru

CivicEye is the dedicated civic grievance platform for the citizens of Tumakuru. Report issues, track resolution in real time, and connect directly with local municipal authorities.

Civic Grievance Categories

Key service areas managed through CivicEye

🛣️

Roads & Footpaths

Report damaged roads, potholes, broken pavements, and pedestrian safety hazards across Tumakuru wards.

🧹

Sanitation & Waste

Uncollected garbage, overflow in public bins, debris accumulation, and civic cleanliness issues.

💡

Street Lighting

Non-functioning streetlights, exposed wiring, dark corridors, and electrical safety concerns.

🚰

Water & Drainage

Pipeline leaks, contaminated water supply, blocked storm-water drains, and sewage overflow.

🌳

Parks & Public Spaces

Maintenance of municipal parks, tree hazards, playground equipment, and public amenities.

📋

Town Planning & Encroachments

Unauthorized construction, public road encroachment, and municipal zoning complaints.

How CivicEye Works

Simple 3-step resolution process for citizens

01

Report Issue

Citizens capture the problem with location and details directly on the portal.

02

Ward Assignment

CivicEye routes the grievance to the respective Tumakuru ward official.

03

Transparent Resolution

Officials address the issue with verification and status updates for the citizen.

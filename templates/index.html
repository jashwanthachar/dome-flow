S2 SMARTER TRAFFIC & ROAD MANAGEMENT ARCHITECTURE

Mathematical Foundations & System Constraints

S2 CONSTRAINT #1 NH-206 Corridor Geographical Scope

Focused on the high-density rural-urban transition corridor on National Highway 206 (CIT Gubbi to Tumakuru). This 22-kilometer arterial features 4 critical bottleneck intersections:

  • J1: CIT Campus Gate — Student transit surge mixed with industrial freight.
  • J2: Gubbi Town Cross — Agricultural produce haulage, tractors & intercity bus bottleneck.
  • J3: Nittur Highway Market — Dense pedestrian market activity & single-lane culvert bottlenecks.
  • J4: Batawadi Entry Junction — Multi-lane toll and industrial entry into Tumakuru city center.
S2 CONSTRAINT #2 Queue Discharge Model & Crowd Telematics Assumptions

Saturation-flow queue-discharge equation:

T_flush = t_react + t_startup + (Σ count_i × PCU_i) × h / effective_lanes

Greenshields describes speed-density relationships for moving traffic, so it was replaced with a stopped-queue discharge estimate. Current assumptions: reaction 3.5 s, start-up lost time 2.0 s, nominal saturation headway 2.0 s/PCU (about 1800 PCU/hr/lane), and 2 effective lanes per arm. PCU factors: car 1.0, two-wheeler 0.5, heavy vehicle 2.2, auto-rickshaw 1.2. Estimates include h=1.7–2.6 s/PCU and ±15% PCU uncertainty. These values require calibration; verify PCU factors against IRC:106 before presenting. The high estimate is used for the timing check; it does not guarantee real-world queue clearance.

Crowd-Telematics Inertial Filter: Filters configured vehicle speeds below 15 km/h or phone jerk below 15 m/s³. Three qualifying phone hits create a demo PWD work-order entry.

Microphone consent

What: the browser reads live microphone samples to calculate siren frequency pattern, confidence, loudness trend, slope and R-squared. Raw samples, recordings, waveforms and spectrograms stay in memory on this device and are not uploaded or saved.

Why: derive a local siren event. Only the derived event fields are sent, signed by the junction-unit demo key.

How long: the microphone is used only while active; signed derived acoustic events are retained on the server for up to 7 days. You can stop the mic or withdraw consent below at any time; withdrawal stops capture and blocks further acoustic requests.

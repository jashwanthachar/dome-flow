S2 CONSTRAINT #1 NH-206 Corridor Geographical Scope

Focused on the high-density rural-urban transition corridor on National Highway 206 (CIT Gubbi to Tumakuru). This 22-kilometer arterial features 4 critical bottleneck intersections:

  • J1: CIT Campus Gate — Student transit surge mixed with industrial freight.
  • J2: Gubbi Town Cross — Agricultural produce haulage, tractors & intercity bus bottleneck.
  • J3: Nittur Highway Market — Dense pedestrian market activity & single-lane culvert bottlenecks.
  • J4: Batawadi Entry Junction — Multi-lane toll and industrial entry into Tumakuru city center.
S2 CONSTRAINT #2 Greenshields Hydrodynamic Model & Crowd Telematics Assumptions

Greenshields Queue-Flush Equation:

T_flush = 3.5 + (Cars × 6.5 / 6.5) + (Trucks × 13.5 / 4.0) + (Bikes × 3.2 / 8.0)

Assumes startup lost time t0 = 3.5s, realistic vehicle lengths, and following headway gaps. Guarantees queue clearance before ambulance arrival (ETA = d / v).

Crowd-Telematics Inertial Filter: Filters configured vehicle speeds below 15 km/h or phone jerk below 15 m/s³. Three qualifying phone hits create a demo PWD work-order entry.

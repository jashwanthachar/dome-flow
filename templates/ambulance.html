Privacy controls

Exact location is sent only while this enrolled vehicle is approved and you have started duty. Stopping duty stops reports. The server keeps the latest position trace for up to 24 hours.

Vehicle report fields: vehicle_id, lat, lon, heading_deg, speed_kmh, timestamp, nonce, signature. Requests also carry the random X-Privacy-Install-ID header. The browser key is stored locally and never sent.
